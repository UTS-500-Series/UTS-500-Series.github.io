Practical — 08

What is left

The circuit is complete. What remains is one wiring fix, some decisions that are cheap now and dear after routing, and the board.

StepWhy it comes here
Put IC2 and IC5 on +16 VTheir V+ pins are on −16 V, so as drawn the module has no output. Two symbols to change (power).
Decide the knob directionsThe mid bands cut and lower their frequency clockwise, the shelves boost and raise it. Swapping pot pins is free before routing (bands).
Settle the panelAs placed, ten shafts come through the panel in one line, 7.5 mm apart. A front board, or a different arrangement, changes the placement (board).
Move the bypass switchIf bypass is a panel control, SW1 has to be at the front of the card, not by J1.
Fix the footprintsRadial footprints for the 100 µF and 10 µF electrolytics; delete or wire J2 and J3.
Decide where the grounds joinPins 5 and 13 are on one net. Choose the single point where supply return meets audio ground.
Consider C33 and the high shelf’s divider22 pF for C33 makes the module flat; the R12/R13 divider is why the high shelf is weaker (shelves). Both optional.
Finish placement, close the outline and routeThen DRC can check the board against the sheet.
Finish the parts listPart numbers for the resistors, the 10k pots, the switch and the chips, and one op amp on both the sheet and the order.
Draw the faceplateFrom the board’s shaft positions, as the compressor’s is drawn from its front board.
