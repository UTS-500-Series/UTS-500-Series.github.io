The panel is the binding constraint

Counting what is drawn: three knobs per band, one each for the high-pass, the low-pass and the gain. That is nine knobs, before any bypass switch. The compressor fits nine functions on the same 38.10 mm faceplate only by using concentric and pull-switch pots, and three of the equaliser’s knobs are dual-gang already, which rules out the simplest concentric pairings. Worth settling on the faceplate before footprints are chosen, because the pot type decides the footprint.
