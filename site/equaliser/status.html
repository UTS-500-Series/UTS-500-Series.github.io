Building it

The module is built and working. Most of the questions this page used to list were settled before ordering; the rest are notes for anyone building another one.

Bringing it up

  1. Before soldering the five op amps, power the board from a bench supply through the card edge and check the rails at their pads: +16 V on pin 4 and −16 V on pin 11 of each OPA1644, pin 7 and pin 4 on U1.
  2. Fit everything, power up with every knob centred and SW1 on the EQ side, and feed a 1 kHz tone. The output should match the input level to within half a dB.
  3. Sweep each boost/cut knob and check its band moves. If pin 3 of each pot is its clockwise end, the mid bands boost anticlockwise (bands).
  4. Flip SW1 to bypass: the output should equal the input exactly, powered or not.

The faceplate

The faceplate isn’t in the repository yet. It needs ten knob holes in two columns from the pot positions above, plus a legend that follows the mid bands’ reversed directions. The compressor’s printed faceplate is the pattern to copy.
