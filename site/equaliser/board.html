Two placement questions to settle before routing

The knobs, as placed, are 7.5 mm apart in a single line. The pots are right-angle parts on the main card, in two rows along its front edge. A right-angle pot puts its shaft at a fixed height above the board, so every shaft comes through the panel on the same line, wherever the pot sits on the card. With the ten pots alternating between the rows, their shafts are 7.5 mm apart, centre to centre. That is too close for any knob larger than about 7 mm, and the back row’s shafts have to pass between the front row’s bodies. Ten knobs in one line on a 133 mm panel get about 12 mm each at best, once the mounting screws have their space. The compressor solved the same problem with a front board behind the panel; the faceplate guide compares the options.

The bypass switch is at the back. SW1 is placed beside J1, at the rack end of the card, so it can’t be reached once the module is in the rack. If bypass is a panel control, it needs to move to the front, as a panel or right-angle toggle.

Footprints to check

  • C18 and C20 (100 µF) are on 5 mm ceramic disc footprints. The parts list has an 8 mm electrolytic can (Jaycar RE6334), which needs a radial footprint like C4’s.
  • C2 and C7 (10 µF) are on a 4 × 2.5 mm film-capacitor footprint. The parts list has bipolar electrolytics (Jaycar RY6810), so these need radial footprints too.
  • J2 and J3, the two 24-pin headers, have no footprint and nothing connected. If they are a leftover, delete them; if they are meant for a front board, they need wiring.
  • The pots use Alps RK097 footprints. The parts list’s dual pot is a Bourns PTD902; check its pins against the RK097 footprint before ordering.

TL084 or OPA1644

The sheet says OPA1644 and the parts list says TL084. They share the quad pinout and both have JFET inputs, so either works in the circuit, and the TL084 comes in TSSOP-14 to fit the footprint. The OPA1644 is about three times quieter (5 nV/√Hz against the TL084’s 18) and drives the 10k loads here more comfortably. Settle on one so the sheet and the order agree.

The dual pot is log taper, and these pages assume linear: on RV1 and RV4, wired as variable resistors, a log track spreads the frequency sweep more evenly across the knob, but it changes where on the travel each corner frequency sits.
