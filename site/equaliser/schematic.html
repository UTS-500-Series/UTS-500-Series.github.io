Within each band the op amp units talk through local labels: A+, A- and Ao are unit A’s inputs and output, and so on. The labels B+, C+, F+ and G+ are drawn on ground, which is right: those are the integrators’ + inputs.

Three things to join before this is a working card

The op amps have no supply. IC2 and IC3 take their rails from VCC and VEE power symbols, but J1 brings in +16V and -16V. KiCad treats differently named power symbols as different nets, so as drawn nothing powers the op amps. Rename the op amps’ symbols to +16V and −16V (or the connector’s to VCC and VEE), and add decoupling: 100 nF from each rail to ground at each op amp, and a bulk capacitor per rail where it comes in.

No audio goes in or out. The low-mid band’s input (the free end of R29 and RV8 pin 1) and the high-mid band’s output Ho end in bare wires. They need to reach J1’s input and output pins, through whatever input and output stages the module will have, using the same pinout as the compressor’s edge connector.

Two grounds. The circuit’s ground goes to pin 13, the rack’s power ground. Pin 5, audio ground, is not connected to anything. Decide which the audio returns to before laying out.
