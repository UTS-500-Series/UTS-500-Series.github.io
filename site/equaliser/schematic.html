Reading it

WhereWhat
Top rowBalanced input receiver (IC1 and the input filtering), then the low-mid band (IC3) and the high-mid band (IC2), each drawn the same way.
MiddleThe buffer (IC4B), the low-pass and high-pass filters (IC4D, IC4C), the variable gain stages (IC5A, IC5B) and the summer (IC5C, with the dry inverter IC5D).
RightThe balanced line driver (IC6) and the bypass switch (SW1).
Bottom leftJ1, the card edge, and the rail filtering. J2 and J3 are 24-pin headers with nothing connected.
BottomIC5 drawn as one block with its supply pins.

Blocks talk through global labels named after the op amp unit they belong to: A+, A- and Ao are unit A’s inputs and output, and so on through P. The labels on ground (B+, C+, F+, G+, M+–P+) are right: those are + inputs that sit at ground.

About this drawing

The sheet is saved by KiCad 9. This image is drawn by the site’s own generator from the .kicad_sch file, and its netlist matches KiCad’s own export net for net. The positions, symbols and wires are the file’s; the lettering is close to KiCad’s but not identical. The PDF KiCad exports is on Design files.

That is the standard pinout, the same as the compressor’s main input and output. One difference: pins 5 and 13 are both on the one ground net here, so the rack’s supply return and audio ground are joined on the card wherever the layout puts them. The compressor keeps them apart and joins them at one point; worth deciding where that join is before the ground tracks go down.
