The equaliser module

A multi-band equaliser for the desk: a small number of bands, each able to lift or cut, on the same 1.5 inch panel as everything else.

Nothing here is designed yet

The equaliser module has no schematic, no netlist and no bill of materials. This section is a template that mirrors the structure of the compressor documentation, so the shape of the work is visible before the work starts. Every figure below is deliberately blank rather than guessed.

The equaliser is the module most constrained by its front panel. Every band needs at least a frequency and a gain control, and a 38 mm wide faceplate runs out of room long before the circuit does — so the panel layout has to be settled before the circuit is worth drawing.

What has to be decided first

These are the choices that determine everything else. Until they are made, the pages in this section have nothing to say.

How this section will be built

The compressor section is generated from a single source of truth: a design.py holding the netlist as data, which the KiCad schematic is verified against pin by pin. The interactive schematics, the component data behind them and the bill-of-materials counts all come from that one file.

This module follows the same route. Nothing here becomes real documentation until there is a design.py to generate it from.
