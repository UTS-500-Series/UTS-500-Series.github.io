The Altronics column gives the catalogue code and how many packs to order, so parts sold in tens are already covered. The whole list is also a spreadsheet: altronics.csv, built from the board files by tools/bom_altronics.py in the compressor repository.

Not from Altronics

  • Film capacitors. Altronics' film range stops at 1 µF on a 5 mm pitch. C5, C8, C15, C9, C10, C14 and C35 are drawn for the TDK and KEMET parts named below, about $64.63 from element14. C15 must be film: an electrolytic leaks and shortens the release.
  • R61 and R62 are 0.1% parts in values Altronics doesn't stock.
  • The twenty 2 mm meter LEDs. Altronics has no 2 mm LEDs, and its 3 mm flangeless ones don't fit the meters' 3.0 mm pitch. Buy 2 mm round flat-tops from element14 or DigiKey.
  • The LM3914 is at Altronics but low stock, and both meters use it now that the LM3915 can't be had; see the meters.
