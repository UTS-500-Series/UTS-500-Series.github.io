Each row gives the shop, its order code (linked to the product page) and how many were bought, so parts sold in packs show the packs. The whole list is also a spreadsheet: ordered.csv, the same file as bom/ordered.csv in the compressor repository.

Altronics came first, for everything it had in stock. DigiKey covered the parts no shop in Sydney sells: the 10 µF and 4.7 µF film capacitors, R61 and R62 at 0.1%, and the 22 µF bipolars that fit the 6.3 mm outline. The op amps, transistors, zener and 100 nF capacitors went on the same order to reach free shipping. Jaycar supplied the rest.

Different from the drawing

  • The meter LEDs are Jaycar's 2 mm tower LEDs, orange in the amber positions. Check that one LED's base is under 3 mm thick before fitting all twenty.
  • C14 and C35 are 2.2 µF bipolar electrolytics (Jaycar RY6804), because the 2.2 µF film part was out of stock everywhere we looked. Neither is in the audio path. They are 5 mm cans on 2.0 mm leads, so spread the leads to the 5 mm holes.
  • The Jaycar resistors are 0.5 W mini metal film, the same size as the 0.25 W parts the board is drawn for.
  • C1 and C2 are 35 V; the 50 V parts don't fit the 6.3 mm outline.
