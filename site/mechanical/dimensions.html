Numbers to design to

These come from the VPR Alliance 500-series drawing and from the panel already worked out in the compressor repository. The spec gives nominal sizes; builders routinely trim the panel slightly so it drops into any rack.

FeatureValue (mm)Source and note
Faceplate width38.10 (1.500″)VPR spec. Make it 37.6 to 37.8 mm in practice so neighbouring modules and powder coat don't bind.
Faceplate height133.35 (5.250″)VPR spec. 132.6 mm (5.22″) is a common practical height.
Faceplate thickness3.18 (0.125″)VPR spec. 3 mm aluminium stock is fine.
Mounting holesØ3.18, csk 82° to Ø5.72VPR spec, two places, countersunk on the front face.
Mounting hole pitch125.43 (4.938″)Compressor panel README. On the vertical centreline, 3.96 mm from each end.
Depth behind panel150.83 ±0.5 (5.938″)VPR spec, from the panel's mounting surface to the rear.
Edge connector15 pin, 3.96 (0.156″) pitchVPR spec, mates with an EDAC 306/307-015-520-102 type socket.
PCB thickness1.6 (0.062″)Standard board; the socket expects it.
PCB offset from panel centrelinecheckPublished figures disagree: 11.13 mm and 7.92 mm both appear on GroupDIY. Take it from the Pre-Amp template board, then confirm against the rack you will use.
Power budget±16 V @ 130 mA, +48 V @ 5 mAVPR spec, per module.

More discussion of practical tolerances is in the GroupDIY mechanical specifications thread.
