Per-module notes

Where each module's mechanical work starts from, as of 28 September 2026.

ModuleWhere it isFirst step
CompressorFull panel defined in panel/make_panel.py, with faceplate.dxf and a dimensioned drawing. Schematic done, no PCB layout.In the faceplate sketch, Insert → Insert DXF the existing faceplate.dxf and extrude from it. Pick one of the three layouts first; the DXF is whichever was generated last.
EqualiserSeparate KiCad projects per filter band. The low band has 15 footprints placed, no traces. No 500-series board or panel yet.Combine the bands onto one board started from the Pre-Amp template, then export STEP. Fix the missing OPA1641 model first.
Pre-AmpThe repository is the Series 500 KiCad template board, 47 footprints. No panel.Export STEP now to get the card and connector envelope into Fusion. It doubles as the reference for the other two boards.

One source of truth per faceplate

If the compressor panel moves into Fusion, treat make_panel.py as retired, or keep the script as master and re-import its DXF whenever it changes. Editing both will drift.
