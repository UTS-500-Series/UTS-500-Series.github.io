Plan the grounds before routing

The compressor has three ground nets on purpose. Keeping them separate on copper is what makes that worthwhile.

NetComes fromHow to lay it out
AGNDJ1 pin 5The audio reference. Make it a copper pour covering most of the bottom layer, so every part has a short, low-impedance path to it.
PGNDJ1 pin 13The rack's power return. It joins AGND at one point only, through R48 (0 Ω). Place R48 near the power entry and route PGND as a single track from pin 13 to it, with no pour.
CHASSISJ1 pin 1The metalwork. It joins AGND through R52 (100 Ω) and C18 (10 nF). Keep both next to pin 1. Chassis also reaches the faceplate through pot bushings and the bracket.

Meter current

The LM3914 and LM3915 drivers switch up to 14 LEDs on and off, which draws pulses of current. Give U9, U10, the LEDs and C38 their own ground track back towards the power entry, and join it to the main pour there. If those pulses flow through the pour under the audio stages, they can be heard as a faint buzz that follows the meters.
