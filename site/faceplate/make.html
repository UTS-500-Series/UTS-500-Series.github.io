Make the panel

Three ways to get a physical panel, from a quick fit check to the real thing.

MethodWhat you sendNotes
Machined aluminiumPanel sketch as DXF (holes and outline only) plus a PDF drawingThe real thing: 3 mm stock, anodised, legends engraved or UV printed. The mechanical guide covers modelling and the drawing.
Panel as a PCBGerbers from a KiCad board that is just the panelCheapest and fastest. Outline on Edge.Cuts, holes as NPTH pads, legends in silkscreen, black mask with white print. Thinner than spec (1.6 to 2.0 mm), which is fine for testing but flexes more. Aluminium-core boards are stiffer.
3D print or laser-cut acrylicSTL or DXFA fit check only. Make one before ordering metal to catch misplaced holes.

Whichever you choose, generate it from the front board layout rather than retyping coordinates. The compressor's panel/make_panel.py is the existing source for its panel. If the front board becomes the master, either update the script's positions to match or retire it, so there's only one source.
