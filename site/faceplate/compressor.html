Compressor notes

Where the compressor stands against this guide, as of 28 September 2026.

  • The committed panel files and panel/README.md describe different layouts. The files show five separate pots plus a LINK toggle (SW4). The README describes dual-concentric knobs and a pull-switch on MAKEUP. Pick one before designing the front board.
  • If you keep dual-concentric pots, RV3/RV4 and RV5/RV6 each become one six-pin part, and the schematic's single RK09K footprints need to change to a concentric footprint from the maker's datasheet.
  • The switches currently use 1.00 mm pin sockets on the main board. With a front board, the switches move there and those sockets become part of a 2.54 mm board-to-board connector.
  • The meter LEDs sit at x = 13.6 and 24.5 in the panel file, 10.9 mm apart. A front board with U9, U10 and 14 LEDs fits comfortably in that area.
