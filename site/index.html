How far along each module is

The compressor has been designed and its boards were ordered on 3 October: a main card and a front board behind the faceplate, joined by a ribbon. The whole circuit has been simulated in ngspice and its values corrected from that, it has a parts list drawn from Altronics' stock, and its faceplate can be 3D printed as well as machined. Its parts have been ordered and it is being built. Its pages are generated from a netlist that the KiCad schematic is verified against, pin by pin, so the figures in it come from the design rather than from memory.

The equaliser's boards were ordered on 8 October and it is being built: a low shelf, two parametric mid bands and a high shelf between a balanced receiver and line driver, on a four-layer main card with a small top board for the frequency pots. Its response is worked out from the schematic and matches the team's LTspice run where they overlap.

The preamp's board has also been ordered and it is being built, with the component values of ESP Project 66 and 96, the published design its schematic redraws. Its pages are written from its KiCad sheet, with ESP's values on our part references.
