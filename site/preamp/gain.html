Planned section 03

The gain stage

Where the 60-odd dB of gain is made, and how it is kept quiet.

Nothing here is designed yet

The preamp module has no schematic, no netlist and no bill of materials. This section is a template that mirrors the structure of the compressor documentation, so the shape of the work is visible before the work starts. Every figure below is deliberately blank rather than guessed.

Open questions

  • How much gain, and is it continuous or switched in steps?
  • Single stage or split across two? A single stage is simpler; two lets the first stage stay at fixed low-noise gain.
  • Whether the same NE5532 and BC549 palette as the compressor is adequate here, or whether the noise target needs a lower-noise input device.

What this page will contain

Once the section is drawn: an interactive schematic of the sheet, a walk through what each part does and why it is that value, and the measurements or calculations behind any figure quoted. The compressor section shows the format.
