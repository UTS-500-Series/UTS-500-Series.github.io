Power and the edge connector

The card takes ±16 V, ground and +48 V from the rack. Its audio does not go through the edge connector at all.

The audio is on flying leads

The microphone comes in on J2–J4 and the output leaves on J5 and J6, all single-pin headers. That works for a bench build, but in the rack the preamp should take its input and send its output through the card edge like the other modules. Wiring J2/J3 and J5/J6 to J1's audio pins, using the same pinout as the compressor's edge connector, is the change that makes it a drop-in 500-series card.

There is no series resistor or filter on either rail. The rack's rails are shared with every other module, and a preamp at high gain amplifies whatever reaches its supply, so a small resistor ahead of C5 and C9 on each rail would be cheap insurance.

Current

The two pairs draw from the rails through R6, R7, R19 and R20; U1 adds its own quiescent current. With no values yet, the total cannot be worked out, but a circuit of this size is far inside the 130 mA per rail a 500-series slot allows. Phantom comes from the separate 48 V rail and does not count against it.
