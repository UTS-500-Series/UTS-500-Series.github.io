Reading it

The sheet is laid out in three blocks:

WhereWhat
Top leftMicrophone input (J2–J4), phantom feed (SW1, C1, R1, R2), DC blocking (C2, C3) and protection (R3, R4, D1–D4). Ends at the global labels +PreIN and -PreIN.
Top rightJ1, the 15-pin card edge, as drawn by the template. The unused pins carry no-connect flags.
BottomThe gain stage (Q1–Q4 and the gain network), the difference amp U1A and the output (R11, R18, J5, J6), with the ±16 V rails across the top and bottom.

The two halves are joined only by global labels: +PreIN and -PreIN carry the protected input down to the transistor bases.

About this drawing

KiCad 10 cannot be installed where the site is built, so this image is drawn by the site's own generator from the .kicad_sch file rather than exported by KiCad. The positions, symbols and wires are the file's; the lettering is close to KiCad's but not identical. The netlist is built the way KiCad joins pins, wires, junctions and labels, and was checked against an independent netlister.
